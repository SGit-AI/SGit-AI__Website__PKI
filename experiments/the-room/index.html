The chain room

The workflow this estate exists to serve, walked end to end: reality → twin → facts → finding on this side of the line, risks → decisions → monitoring on the other. Pick a verb, then a station. Or

The left half of this room is real — every desk stands on a fetchable artefact: the measured library entry, the signed mandate, the computed excess row. The right half is SYNTHETIC and marked on every surface: no risk has been derived, priced, accepted or monitored by anybody. The workflow is simulated first, then supported — the same way this register taught its shape with ten labelled fixtures before one real record existed. Fixture: instance-fixture.synthetic.json.

The room
Every word spoken here is derived at build time from the same files the pipeline runs on — nothing about state is hand-written. Click a verb, then a desk. The boundary is clickable too.
The walk, as text (the same eight stops the button plays)
  1. The environment enters: claude-code-remote, as installed on 2026-08-26. the library entry
  2. Measured from inside: 9 nodes, 2 unknown — the self-measurement probe was refused. A floor, not a census. the measurement method
  3. Registered: the grant joins 23 signed statements in 11 records. The counter has no opinion about any of them. the register's front door
  4. Authorised: mandate v2 — claude/**, dev, expiring 2026-12-31. Somebody decided, and signed. the signed mandate
  5. Computed on arrival: grant 41 resources − mandate 1 → excess 40. Acceptor: none — nobody has accepted this, yet. the excess row
  6. The finding crosses as a REFERENCE — a library identifier, not a copy. Personal data never crosses the other way. the contract (book ch. 12)
  7. SYNTHETIC · illustrative — Risk 58 · medium is derived and ACCEPTED — by Project Lead, sgit, for 126 days, reviewed by Site agent (drafting) + Project Lead (deciding). The exposure has an owner for the first time in the chain. the instance fixture (synthetic)
  8. SYNTHETIC · illustrative — Monitored: 3 of 4 conditions holding; c4 has never held (the hook is tier setting; the boundary version is proposed and unbuilt (GM-D26)). In 126 days the interval ends and the item walks back across the line: re-measured, re-computed, re-decided. the conditions (synthetic)

What this room does not claim. The right half is invented, and labelled — it teaches the workflow's shape and is not evidence the shape works. The genre is the point-and-click adventure's grammar with this estate's own sentences, figures, palette and verbs, ported from newsroom.sgit.ai's debrief (CC BY 4.0) whose own limits carry over: the genre has one prior implementation, unread by users, untested with screen readers. And the room opens none of the nine shut doors — it makes one of them, nobody has ever accepted an exposure, explainable to the person who might.

Specified in brief v0.33.66 · generator admin/build/gen_room.py · gates: route = declared chain; the fixture stores references, never copies (GM3); a decision carries a named acceptor and an interval; a condition's status is observed, never typed; the synthetic marker travels with every quote.
