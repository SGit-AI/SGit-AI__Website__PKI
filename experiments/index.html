Experiments

Game-like environments for the workflows this estate serves — simulated first, then supported. One experiment, one folder, one workflow, one visualisation, each with its own generator, its own gates, and its own bench entry saying what it does not prove.

every card is a node in that library entry, gated one-to-one · brief v0.33.68 The Deploy Workflow: the environment a permitted push lands in — a CI runner with no agent, no hooks, and one platform-enforced grant Visualisation: the same engine, a different scenario.json: nothing else changed, which is the claim the pair exists to prove Honesty: one hundred per cent real — the twin is the estate's CI runner, measured from inside by the same tool on the same day · brief v0.33.68 The control room Workflow: both worlds at once, as an operator sees a plant — state at a glance, and the 26 August incident replayable on a sequence-of-events log Visualisation: a SCADA board with a game HUD: mimic diagrams, annunciator tiles lit by tier, faceplates on click, transport controls over the replay Honesty: one hundred per cent real, and zero new data — a second renderer over the same scenario.json worlds, every verdict re-run through mandate.py at build, mode chip pinned to REPLAY · brief v0.33.69

The rules every experiment inherits

  1. Every word derived at build time from the files the pipeline runs on; no typed counts.
  2. Real and synthetic never blend — a surface is derived from real artefacts, or it carries the SYNTHETIC marker beside every value.
  3. Every visual claim has a gate comparing the drawing to the declaration.
  4. Genre, not work: a genre's conventions are for using; a specific game's art, names or wording are not.
  5. Render it, screenshot it, read the screenshot before shipping.

Manifest: experiments.json — the hub is generated from it, and a manifest entry without a folder (or a folder without an entry) fails the build. Convention declared in brief v0.33.67.

pki.sgit.ai

A register of people and their public keys: verified by an anchor first, then by each other, with a link that only counts when it goes both ways; and the published work behind it.
