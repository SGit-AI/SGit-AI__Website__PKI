Build your own

PlayThe meshReportSources & correctionsBuild your ownThe probes

This is what you give to your agent. One link, to a brief that points at the pages and the data on this site rather than repeating them, and asks the agent to design and build its own version of the game — its idea, its style, its interface, its technology — within twelve rules that are not its to change. Hand the same link to several agents and compare what comes back.

the hand-off

Copy this link and give it to the agent

https://pki.sgit.ai/guess/prompt.md open the raw markdown

Or paste this line

Read https://pki.sgit.ai/guess/prompt.md and everything it points at. Then do step 1 only: propose your plan for your own version of the game and stop for my review. If I ask for variations, make each genuinely different in genre and technology.

For three variations, add: Give me three variations.

How the exchange goes

  1. Step one, the plan. The agent reads the pages and the data, and replies with a short plan: the idea and its name, the genre and the technology, the screens, a twelve-line table saying how each rule is met, how the data is loaded, what it will not build, the risks. Then it stops.
  2. You review. Pick the variation, change what you want, say go.
  3. Step two, the build. The agent delivers a self-contained folder under guess/variants/<name>/: the game as a static folder, a README with a does-not-prove list, its own self-test output, and a screenshot of the reveal. As a pull request if it can, a zip if it cannot.
  4. We check the seven things the brief lists: it runs from a folder, the self-test passes, nothing leaves the browser, the three classes are distinct, the reveal shows the gap with the fix and the tier caveat, the words, the source links.

The rules are the game's invariants from brief v0.33.64 and brief v0.33.65: a deterministic core, two question classes, a reliability per question, the prediction before the reveal, the gap per capability with its reach and the reduction beside it, three classes never mixed, honest failure, the tier caveat, nothing leaving the browser, every node linked to its source, and the words. Everything else — genre, look, technology, voice, pacing — is the agent's.

The brief, rendered

Variants

None yet. The first agent to hand one back lands under guess/variants/, and this list is where they will be compared: genre, technology, whether the self-test passed, and what each says it does not prove.

What this does not prove

  • That an agent will keep the rules. The brief states them; the checks are ours to run on what comes back.
  • That the variants are comparable measurements. They are explorations of form over one data set and one engine contract; the data and the self-test are the fixed points.

pki.sgit.ai

A register of people and their public keys: verified by an anchor first, then by each other, with a link that only counts when it goes both ways; and the published work behind it. Part of the sgit.ai network — the vault layer and the shipped CLI this registry is designed onto. All content CC BY 4.0.
