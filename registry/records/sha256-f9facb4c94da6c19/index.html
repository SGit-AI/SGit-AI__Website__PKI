pki.sgit.ai

A register of people and their public keys: verified by an anchor first, then by each other, with a link that only counts when it goes both ways; and the published work behind it. Part of the sgit.ai network — the vault layer and the shipped CLI this registry is designed onto. All content CC BY 4.0.
