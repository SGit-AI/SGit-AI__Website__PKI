Eight diagrams, each stating what it claims and which document it comes from: the three-sites composition the registry sits inside (from the v0.33.61 access report), the registry tree, the record as a hash chain, the enrolment write path, the verification walk, the three-session demo, grant/mandate/excess authority (from v0.33.61, folded in via change control), and the fixture-or-identity decision with the flag read before the signature.
