Synthetic readers

The instrument that tests the pages, kept deliberately outside the pack because it feeds the pack rather than belonging to it. Two agents: one is a browser and nothing else, one is an archetype that receives pixels and nothing else and points at where to go next. Patience is set from outside the model, so abandonment is a measured event rather than a story. Findings flow one way — into the pack's change control.

Everything in the run records is simulated. No person said any of it. Synthetic readers reliably find defects — a page that does not answer its own question, a term used before it is defined, a dead end — and cannot report preferences. Those need people, and no person has yet run this protocol, so there is no calibration record and nothing here is known to predict what a human would do.

The three archetypes

Property lists, not portraits — composed so that they map to no individual, with the sources recorded nowhere. The full definitions are in the archetypes document.

R1 — The shipping founder

Ships product with AI coding agents every day. A power user of agents who has never once looked at what they can reach. Turned the permission prompts off early because they interrupted.

Grant position
position 3 → 4 · vibe coding drifting into professional development
Prior confidence
“My repo, basically” — said with more confidence than has been tested
Predicted failure
Bounces off jargon before reaching the finding

R2 — The agent-security practitioner

Secures agent deployments for a living, and runs more agents than the people they advise. Arrives already holding opinions, and stops reading at the first loose word.

Grant position
position 4 · CLI agent, code-host token, several machines
Prior confidence
High and partly earned — has actually looked, though not recently and not everywhere
Predicted failure
Rejects the whole instrument over one inaccurate claim

R3 — The adoption executive

Pushing agent adoption down through a team, measured on productivity rather than on security. Expects a security argument aimed at slowing the thing they are championing.

Grant position
position 1–2 personally, responsible for a team spanning 1 to 5
Prior confidence
Has not thought about it as a question with an answer
Predicted failure
Reads it as an argument against the thing they are championing
