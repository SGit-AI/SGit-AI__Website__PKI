# Appendix — Change Control

**pack** Map Your Case · draft-1 · 21 August 2026
**role** Every correction and every decision in one place. Sources stay verbatim (supersede, never rewrite); this page is where changes live until draft-2 folds them in. It sits last because it never stops growing.

---

## The rule this page implements

The corpus's own: **a published document is not silently edited.** This pack was born with history — the tool shipped twice before the pack existed — so unlike its sibling, this appendix opens with entries rather than waiting for them. Corrections are numbered MC1…; decisions MC-D1…. Where a decision was actually taken in the registry pack's register (because the tool lived there first), the cross-reference is given and the decision is not renumbered as new authority.

## Corrections

### MC1 — Two 20 August briefs are the pack's programme documents

**Source:** `v0.33.61__dev-brief__levels-and-variants-are-two-axes…` and `v0.33.61__dev-brief__screenshot-boundary-is-the-instrument…`, both processed on 21 August.
**What they correct:** the memos they respond to, in ways this pack inherits as design rather than re-derives — levels conflated with variants (separated: a grid, not a ladder); expertise treated as awareness (severed: everybody starts at level one); personas ordered by job title (reordered: by grant size); patience budgets generated by the model (made exogenous); pages generated during a run (fixed artefacts only); personas from named individuals (archetypes only, with the recognisability test).
**Status:** operationalised as documents 07 and 08. The briefs are captured verbatim under `/briefs/` with reader pages under `/documents/`.

### MC2 — The pack is written after the build, and says so

**The correction to the usual reading order:** documents 01–04 describe what exists (capture); 07–09 and 11 describe what does not (specification); 05–06 are mixed and their feature/status columns say which is which. Where pack and tool disagree, the pack is the v3 intent, except where this appendix records the disagreement as deliberate.
**Currently recorded disagreements:** none — v2 and the pack were written by the same hand on consecutive days. The row exists because that convergence will not survive the first outside contribution.
**Status:** standing.

### MC3 — The library is dated as a whole, and that is a defect

**Source:** the registry pack's document 12 (each node carries its own date; a tree dated as a whole is quietly wrong while looking current), applied to this tool's own library.
**What it corrects:** `library.json` at v2 carries one `version` date. A vendor changing one default invalidates one row, not the file — and the sharing design (document 09) needs per-entry dates to say how much a pinned share has drifted.
**Status:** adopted; build-order phase 1.

### MC4 — The copy-summary is superseded by the fragment share

**What v2 shipped:** a clipboard text summary as the sharing story.
**What supersedes it:** document 09's fragment links — same rule (choices, never descriptions), stronger form (reconstructable, pinned, checkable). The copy-summary stays until phase 3 ships, then remains as the plain-text fallback.
**Status:** adopted for v3.

## How this register relates to the registry pack's

The tool grew up inside the registry pack (its document 14, corrections C20–C25, decisions 29–35 and 40–41). Those entries remain the record of *when and by whom* the early decisions were taken; this register carries them forward as MC-D1…MC-D4 for the reader who starts here, and adds what belongs only to this pack. Nothing is renumbered into new authority, and a future correction to a shared decision lands in **both** registers with a cross-reference.
