pki.sgit.ai / book

A Key Means Nothing Alone

One volume explaining what this site actually built, why the concepts underneath it are shaped the way they are, how it composes with RiskMandate.ai — and, as an equal partner to all of that, what none of it proves. It is commissioned and not yet written. The commissioning brief is published here first, which is this estate's habit: the specification goes up before the thing exists, so the thing can be checked against it.

Status

Commissioned · not yet written. The brief is complete enough to hand to a fresh writing session: fifteen chapters in five parts, twelve figures with a hash gate that breaks the build when a screenshot stops matching the page it shows, a chapter written as a contract with the RiskMandate team, and an acceptance test that fails the book if a reader finishes believing the register is trustworthy.

What it covers

PartWhat it does
One — Why there is nothing to inheritIdentity is not authorisation and authorisation is not enforcement; the 2019 flood and the four rules it produced; the bootstrap trap
Two — The vocabularyGrant against mandate; the delta and its three directions; boundary, setting and expectation; reality before the risk register
Three — What was builtThe register, the measurement, the refused push, the building blocks — the part the screenshots serve
Four — How it composesThe library and the instance, the two paths, and eight releases in four days told through what each one learned
Five — Honesty, and a first stepWhat ships, what is argued — the chapter that decides whether the other fourteen can be trusted — and your first mandate, tomorrow

Why the brief is published before the book

The same reason the four rules were published before the registry existed: a specification that appears after the thing it describes cannot be used to check it. The brief names the acceptance test, the honesty positions that must travel inside the chapters rather than in an appendix, and the twelve figures with the gate that keeps them honest. If the finished book misses any of those, this page is the evidence.

The positions the book must carry, stated here too, because a commissioning page that omitted them would be doing the thing the book is not allowed to do: the register is built and the trustworthy register is not — ten of its eleven records are fixtures whose private keys are published, so every signature verifies and proves nothing; the root is a fixture and says so; the hook's enforcement is real and its authority is not; every measured grant is a floor rather than a census; and all of it generalises from two environments, one agent and one mandate.

The model it follows

The shape is borrowed from the sibling estate's book round — a writing round rather than a build round, one book per fresh session, markdown as the source of truth, a PDF that reads start to finish with no link followed, a machine surface carrying hashes, and the honesty positions travelling with the text. What is borrowed is the discipline, not the content.
