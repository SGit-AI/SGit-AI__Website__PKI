pki.sgit.ai / book

A Key Means Nothing Alone

One volume explaining what this site actually built, why the concepts underneath it are shaped the way they are, how it composes with RiskMandate.ai — and, as an equal partner to all of that, what none of it proves. It is commissioned and not yet written. The commissioning brief is published here first, which is this estate's habit: the specification goes up before the thing exists, so the thing can be checked against it.

Status

Commissioned · not yet written. The brief is complete enough to hand to a fresh writing session: seventeen chapters in five parts, twelve figures each taken from the release tag its caption names, a chapter written as a contract with the RiskMandate team, and an acceptance test that fails the book if a reader finishes believing the register is trustworthy.

Draft-2, revised the same day. The sibling estate finished all three of its commissioned books hours after draft-1 was published, and two of them carry disciplines stronger than what this brief had specified. Rather than leave the weaker version standing, the brief was tightened three times over: figures are now time-travelled to the version they claim to show rather than photographed today; every load-bearing claim about what this estate means must be marked stated or drawn, with a gate that re-reads each quotation out of the source it names; and a chapter was added for where the estate contradicts itself and what it does not say. Draft-1 is superseded, not deleted — it is in this file's history at tag v0.1.33.

Why the brief is published before the book

The same reason the four rules were published before the registry existed: a specification that appears after the thing it describes cannot be used to check it. The brief names the acceptance test, the honesty positions that must travel inside the chapters rather than in an appendix, and the twelve figures with the gates that keep them honest. If the finished book misses any of those, this page is the evidence.

Why a figure is taken from the past rather than of it

Several figures the book needs show pages as they were: the push was refused at v0.1.28, and the register shipped at v0.1.26 and has changed since. A picture captioned the register on the day it shipped that was in fact photographed today is a reconstruction — and a reconstruction wearing a caption is a claim of authority nobody granted, which is the exact thing this book is about. So the brief requires the harness instead: check the tag out into a git worktree, serve it on a port used once and never again, photograph it, and tear both down whether the capture worked or not. A figure of the past is then pinned to a tag that does not move and never goes stale; a figure of the present breaks the build on the next release. Two maintenance costs rather than none, taken on purpose.

The positions the book must carry, stated here too, because a commissioning page that omitted them would be doing the thing the book is not allowed to do: the register is built and the trustworthy register is not — ten of its eleven records are fixtures whose private keys are published, so every signature verifies and proves nothing; the root is a fixture and says so; the hook's enforcement is real and its authority is not; every measured grant is a floor rather than a census; and all of it generalises from two environments, one agent and one mandate.

The model it follows

The shape is borrowed from the sibling estate's book round — a writing round rather than a build round, one book per fresh session, markdown as the source of truth, a PDF that reads start to finish with no link followed, a machine surface carrying hashes, and the honesty positions travelling with the text. All three of those books are now finished, and draft-2 borrows two more things from the two that landed last: the making-of volume's time-travel harness, which re-took every one of its twenty figures from the tag its caption names, and the atlas volume's provenance rule — it carries 17 connections anchored in a corpus quote against 151 authored by the writing session, and tells its reader in the front matter not to mistake the second kind for the first. What is borrowed is the discipline, not the content.
