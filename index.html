Public key infrastructure · agent identity · mandates

Good public key repositories
existed, and were destroyed

In 2019 the global keyserver network was flooded until importing a poisoned certificate broke your installation. Its own maintainer called it unsalvageable — and the cause was a design goal stated at the outset, not a bug. Any registry proposed after that should be able to show it was designed with the history in hand.
