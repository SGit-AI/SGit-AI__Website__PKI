  • Provenance: whose these are, and what class of evidence they carry
  • The concordance: where the partner's work meets the doctrine

What this folder does not prove

  • That any market claim in these artefacts is accurate. Not one has been checked against a primary source.
  • That the survey has been run. Somebody else's survey has been RECEIVED, which is a different thing, and collapsing the two would be the first dishonesty in this folder.
  • That the convergences are independent confirmation. Both bodies of work come from two people who met the day before, in one week, with model assistance - so agreement here is weaker evidence than it looks.
  • That the scale collision is resolved. The partner's Index is 0-100; GM-D54 settled 1-5 and explicitly refused 1-100. The concordance sketches a reconciliation and does not make one.
  • That the primitive mapping survives contact. Four of five primitives match on paper; nobody has scored one real placement both ways.
  • That either positioning is right. Neither side has a customer who has paid for a rating.
